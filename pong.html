Power-up guide
  • Big Paddle: enlarge your paddle temporarily.
  • Paddle Boost: move your paddle faster temporarily.
  • Slow Ball: slow the ball for both players.
  • Speed Ball: speed up the ball for both players.
  • Curve Ball: bend the ball's path temporarily.
  • Shield: block one goal against you.
  • Shrink Opponent: shrink the other player's paddle temporarily.
